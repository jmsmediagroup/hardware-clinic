# Commercial & redistribution licensing

Hardware Clinic is free for personal, non-commercial use (see LICENSE). Anything beyond that needs a licence. That includes:

- Using it in a repair shop, IT department, refurbisher, or managed-service provider
- Any paid service where you run it on customers' machines
- Bundling or redistributing the image, or putting it on sticks you sell or hand out
- Building it into your own product or workflow

## What a commercial licence adds
- The right to use it commercially and to put it on your bench sticks
- Signed builds that boot with **Secure Boot enabled** (no BIOS changes on customer machines)
- The **fleet / unattended** features and network upload for volume work
- Priority updates and email support
